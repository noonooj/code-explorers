Programming & AI fundamentals for kids — built for confidence.

Two year-long remote programs with one class per week. Students learn by building projects, practicing problem-solving, and exploring real-world AI concepts at the right level.

Grades 2–5: Programming + AI Foundations (Scratch / Logo)

A gentle, creative introduction to coding and AI ideas using visual tools and beginner-friendly logic.

1 class/week • 1 year
  • Core skills Sequencing, loops, conditionals, events, debugging, and computational thinking.
  • Tools Scratch + Logo-style thinking for logic, shapes, patterns, and storytelling.
  • AI basics What AI is, how data works, patterns, fairness, and safe/ethical AI habits.
  • Capstone projects Games, animations, interactive stories, and simple “smart” projects (high-level).

Grades 6–10: Python + AI Fundamentals

A practical pathway into Python, data thinking, and beginner AI workflows through hands-on mini builds.

1 class/week • 1 year
  • Python foundations Variables, types, conditionals, loops, functions, lists/dictionaries, and clean coding.
  • Projects-first learning Short builds that teach problem-solving, debugging, and teamwork habits.
  • AI fundamentals Data, features, training vs testing, simple models (high-level), and responsible AI.
  • Capstone projects Python apps + beginner AI mini-projects (high-level) with a showcase at the end.
